How It Works

From request to resolution
in three simple steps.

No training required. Works on any Android or iPhone.

📲
1

Homeowner submits a request

Via the HOAHub mobile app — gate pass, document, dues payment, or anything the HOA offers. Takes under two minutes.

🖥️
2

HOA staff reviews & processes

Staff log in to the web dashboard, see all pending requests in one place, and act on them — no more digging through chats or logbooks.

Homeowner gets notified instantly

Approval, denial, or status update — pushed straight to the homeowner's phone. They receive their document or approval digitally.
